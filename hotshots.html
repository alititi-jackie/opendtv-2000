Apple, Matrox and Pinnacle

At a Monday morning press conference Apple announced that Matrox has developed its first
Desktop Video product for the Mac platform. The $999 RTMac currently supports dual stream
DV editing with Final Cut Pro...MPEG-2 support is inherent via the use of the C-Cube DVXpress
chip and will be added as the product develops.

Apple also announced that they are working with Pinnacle and will deliver a resolution independent
nonlinear editing system for SDTV and HDTV called Targa Cine'. The basic product supports multiple
streams of uncompressed SDTV. The enhance version support one stream of uncompressed HDTV
at resolutions up to 1920 x 1080 @24/30P. It also support all 720P & 480P formats and
many formats not found in Table 3.

 

Final Cut Pro shown playing a 1920 x 1080 @ 24P uncompressed stream via the Targa Cine' board.
A complete system with a PowerMac G4, Cine' and storage will sell for about $30,000; it should be
avaialable this summer or fall. DLP Projectors provided the large bright displays.

 

 

A list of Targa Cine' features at the demo in the Pinnacle booth. The board also handles SDTV
upconversions with de-interlacing and proper scaling filters so that SD and HD souce can be mixed.
SMPTE-292m is the interconnect for getting HD source in and out.

 

Dream Camera from Philips

 

A view of the Philips LDK 7000HD Camera The chart below tells some of the story. The LDK 6000HD
is on the camera stand producing 720P and 1080P images. And the LDK 7000HD even supports
REAL widescreen at 2.67:1 with real vertical resolution. Note also that onboard film files allow the camera
to emulate the look of many film stocks. This is a must see!
